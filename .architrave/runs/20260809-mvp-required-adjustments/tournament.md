# Tournament of Options

## Option A — Keep Calendar Reconciliation In MVP

Proves continuity and execution together, but delays product learning behind write consent, ownership, approval, idempotency, and reconciliation.

## Option B — Read-Only MVP, Earn MVP+1

Run Phase -1, prove evidence-backed continuity read-only, then require a STOP/PIVOT/CONTINUE decision before calendar writes.

## Option C — Full Personal-World Substrate

Implements long-term abstractions before proving the appointment pain or compounding value.

## Decision Matrix

| Option | Learning speed | Risk | Product-thesis fit | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Calendar writes in MVP | Low | High | Mixed continuity/execution | Reject |
| Read-only MVP then MVP+1 | High | Low | Direct continuity test | Choose |
| Full substrate | Low | Very high | Speculative | Reject |

## Winner

Option B. Read-only continuity is the actual MVP. Calendar reconciliation is MVP+1 only after demonstrated user value.
