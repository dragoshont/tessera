# Tessera North Star

## Purpose

Tessera is a durable personal intelligence and execution substrate. It keeps user-owned, evidence-backed continuity independent of any model, agent, provider, or chat session, then exposes narrowly authorized capabilities over that state.

The durable product promise is not "an agent that remembers." It is:

> Tessera preserves accepted current state, history, provenance, corrections, and action outcomes so replaceable workers can help without becoming the owner of identity, memory, policy, or authority.

## Current Product Proof

The R0 Appointment experiment in [the product MVP audit](../product-mvp-audit.md) is
historical input, not the controlling R1 vertical. R1 uses the workflow-specific,
synthetic FollowUp continuity proof selected in
[the R1 decision](r1/CONTINUITY_VERTICAL_DECISION.md). This proves mechanics only:
no provider writes, cloud model, broad ontology, generalized autonomy, or permanent
product category is established.

## R0 Role

R0 supplies constrained internal infrastructure: canonical ownership, evidence, append-oriented events, supported current state, bounded context, replaceable workers, versioned capabilities, durable action state, and owner-scoped SQLite persistence.

Generic `AssertionRecord` exists only to distinguish evidence-backed current, conflicted, and historical state. It is not product validation, a user-facing ontology, a graph, a universal Claim model, or permission to introduce Entity, Situation, Commitment, relationship inference, or semantic retrieval.

## Invariants

- The user and Tessera-owned durable state are central; workers are replaceable.
- Evidence, observation, assertion, action, provider verification, and external confirmation remain distinct.
- Policy and authorization remain deterministic and outside model output.
- The current broker is preserved as Tessera's trust plane.
- Long-running state survives process and worker-session loss.
- Context is derived, bounded, sensitivity-filtered, and non-canonical.
- Structure is earned by measured workflow need.
- No current document authorizes a provider integration, cloud model, or live external write.

## Success Boundary

R0 succeeds when its contracts are durable, owner-scoped, restart-safe, and fail
closed. R1 succeeds when corrected accepted context improves deterministic later
interpretation across restart. Product/market success remains unvalidated.
